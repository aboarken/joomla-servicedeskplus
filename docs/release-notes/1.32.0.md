# com_servicedeskplus 1.32.0

Update from **System → Update → Extensions**, or install `com_servicedeskplus.zip` (attached below). No database changes.

## Changed
- **Find Endpoints shows the answer first.** One *Find Endpoints* button (on the page or in the toolbar) runs every check and shows:
  - warnings;
  - a **Server** card and an **API key** card;
  - the **endpoints the portal uses**, each with its result and time;
  - a **Requester** card when an e-mail is given;
  - the **Diagnostics report** to copy.
- The other known endpoints and **Check an Endpoint** are under **Advanced**.
